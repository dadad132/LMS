Our Origins

A Community Created Out of Real Struggles

My journey started exactly like yours: with a TEFL course and big dreams. I was told a certificate was all it took. But I quickly realized that teaching online was far more complex—with platforms to navigate, skills to learn, and no one there to guide me.

In the classroom, my students called me "Teacher Honey". As I slowly figured out the ropes, other teachers began reaching out to me for advice. What started as friendly tips soon blossomed into active mentoring, and Honeypot was born.

Today, Honeypot has grown into a global, supportive family with a dedicated team of training coaches who are absolutely passionate about guiding you step-by-step.

Our Mission

To empower online teachers with practical skills, classroom confidence, and honest mentoring in a community where no teacher feels alone.

Our Vision

To build a global family of confident, highly equipped educators enjoying sustainable and rewarding online teaching careers from anywhere.
